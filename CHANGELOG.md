# Changelog

Every release lists the icons that were added, renamed, removed, redrawn or moved to another category since the release before.

## 5.4.0

A new Energy category with power, meters, plugs and fuses, generation, the grid, heating and charging, including 18 icons that move over from other categories. Houses get corner badges and signs for smart home, solar and heating, the home gets appliances from oven to robot vacuum, and there are cranes, excavators, bridges, currency coins, dashed play and pause progress, thermometers and droplets. The Duotone tone now ends on the centerline of every line, and the houses, buildings, server badges, zap and a few Bulk fills are redrawn.

Search in the CLI and the MCP server now corrects a typo like `calender` when no icon matches and names the corrected search, the same search as on coolicons.cool.

1,751 icons with 14 aliases in 31 categories.
